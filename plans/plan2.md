# Hero Section - Interactive Visual

**Goal**: Create a novel, beautiful, interactive graphic as the hero section of the portfolio.

**Vision**: Simple pieces creating complex emergent structure - like lying under a tree on a spring day, watching leaves fall through sunlight.

---

## Concept

### Inspiration
Lying under a tree on a beautiful sunny spring day on the softest grass possible. The warmth of the sun on your face. Leaves falling around you, playing with the sunlight. A peaceful nap. Beauty from simplicity.

### Core Principles
- **Simple pieces, complex structure**: Like leaves on a tree - individual elements are simple, but the overall pattern is beautiful and complex
- **Emergent complexity**: The magic comes from how simple pieces interact and compose
- **Interactive**: Responds to user interaction (hover, scroll, click, mouse movement)
- **Peaceful & beautiful**: Creates a sense of calm and wonder

### Possible Elements
- Stars
- Shapes (geometric primitives)
- Fractals
- Constellation patterns
- Particle systems
- Falling/floating elements
- Light and shadow play

### Mood & Feel
- Peaceful
- Mesmerizing
- Beautiful
- Warm
- Natural
- Organic complexity from simple rules

---

## Technical Considerations

### Technologies to Explore
- Canvas API for performance
- WebGL/Three.js for 3D effects
- SVG for crisp vector graphics
- CSS animations for lightweight interactions
- React Spring or Framer Motion for smooth animations

### Performance
- Must be performant on all devices
- Consider reduced motion preferences
- Graceful degradation for older devices

### Responsiveness
- Adapts to different screen sizes
- Works on mobile (touch interactions)
- Maintains visual impact across devices

## Implementation Ideas

### Option 1: Falling Leaves/Particles
- Particles drift down the screen
- Interact with cursor/touch
- Play with light/shadow
- Parallax effect on scroll

### Option 2: Constellation/Star Field
- Interactive star field
- Connect dots based on proximity to cursor
- Constellations form and dissolve
- Depth with parallax

### Option 3: Fractal Growth
- Organic fractal patterns
- Grows/responds to interaction
- Tree-like or neural network aesthetic
- Morphs over time

### Option 4: Particle Swarm
- Boids algorithm (flocking behavior)
- Particles respond to cursor
- Emergent patterns from simple rules
- Beautiful, organic movement
